TWO DEVICES. ONE LOCAL NETWORK.

Make the introduction.

Bring a Mac running macOS 26 or later and an Android 11+ phone. Keep them on the same reachable local network.

  1. 1

    Download Nearby.

    Open the downloaded DMG and drag Nearby to Applications. Open it from there. The app includes its Android tools, so there’s nothing to build.

    Installation & first-open guide ↗
  2. 2

    Say hello.

    Enable Wireless debugging in your phone’s Developer options. Choose Pair Device in Nearby, then scan its QR code or enter a pairing code.

  3. 3

    Make room.

    Allow Local Network access on your Mac. Choose Mirror when your phone appears, and settle it into your desktop.

A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

The useful details.

Small print, in a readable size.

What do I need to connect?

macOS 26 or later, an Android 11+ phone, and a shared local network with Wireless debugging enabled. Nearby supports QR and code pairing, with manual addresses available in Connection Help. Guest Wi-Fi, network isolation, and VPN rules can prevent discovery.

Why does macOS warn when I open Nearby?

This alpha is ad-hoc signed, without a Developer ID certificate or Apple notarization. macOS cannot verify the developer or check this release through notarization. If you trust the release, follow the app-specific first-open steps in the installation guide. Nearby supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 26 or later.

Does it use a cloud service?

Mirroring, text clipboard sharing and file uploads use the local connection between your Mac and paired Android phone. Nearby reuses your phone’s ADB authorization and runs its own wireless-only ADB service on your Mac.

What can I share?

Text can move between your Mac and phone. Regular files can be uploaded from your Mac to the phone’s Downloads folder; folders, aliases and symbolic links aren’t supported. Android file uploads require an arm64 or x86-64 device. Clipboard images and audio mirroring aren’t implemented.

Can it mirror every Android screen?

Android can hide secure or protected content from mirroring. Authentication may still need to happen on the phone, and individual apps may decline pasted text. Keep Wireless debugging enabled while using Nearby.

Where can I report an issue or contribute?

Start with the GitHub repository for source, build instructions, architecture and verification notes. For a bug or idea, open an issue with the steps and your device and macOS versions. Keep personal data and clipboard contents out of reports.
